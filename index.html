01 / GRADES

Cosmetic grades

These labels describe the cosmetic grade or grade mix of a lot.

ABC+
A mix of A, B and C grades.
AB+
A mix of A and B grades.
B+
B grade.
BC+
A mix of B and C grades.

The + is part of the grade name. It does not specify a mix percentage or an additional technical guarantee.

02 / BIDDING

Place your daily bids

  1. Find your model

    Search by model or storage, or use the brand and VAT filters.

  2. Enter your price

    Enter a bid in the relevant grade column. All bids are in EUR per device.

  3. Save your bids

    You can edit several prices and save them together using the bar at the bottom.

  4. Update before 16:00

    Saved bids can be edited while the auction is open. Bidding is blocked when it closes.

03 / QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Can I see other buyers’ bids?

No. Bids are private. The auction page shows yesterday’s final price as a reference, alongside your own bid.

What does “Yesterday” mean?

It is the previous day’s final price for that model and grade. It is a reference price, not the current highest bid.

Where can I find my saved bids?

Open My Bids in the sidebar. You can also export saved bids as a CSV file using “Export my bids”.

Why can’t I see the number of devices?

Device quantities are not displayed in the auction. You enter a price per device for the selected model and grade.

Are these real bids?

This is a clickable prototype with sample data. Bids are saved only in your current browser and are not submitted as real orders. The countdown and open/closed selector are for demonstration.
